Practical Application of AI in Tourism · Week 9

Build Your Name Card App

You give Gemini a few keywords. Gemini builds a working app. You publish it on the internet with Netlify. Anyone can open your app on a phone and make a name card.

Basic mission

  • Build your name card app in Gemini Canvas, with a design toolkit and a design from a photo (Steps 1 to 7).
  • Publish it with Netlify and get your link (Steps 8 to 10).
  • Make one name card with your app and post it on Padlet (Step 11).

Challenge mission

  • Ask Gemini for one more feature (Step 7, second request).
  • Give your Netlify project a better name (Step 10).

Request 5: "I want this design"

Show Gemini a card design you like. Gemini changes your app to that style.

  1. Find one name card design you like. Search Google Images for business card design minimal (or elegant, colorful, travel). Right click the picture, Save image as. Or take a photo of a paper card.
  2. In the same Gemini chat, press + → Files (파일) and choose your picture. You can also drag the picture into the chat box.
  3. Copy Request 5, paste it next to the picture, send.
REQUEST 5

8

Download your app file

  1. At the top of the app panel, press Export (내보내기), then Download code (코드 다운로드).
  2. Open your Downloads folder. Find the new .html file.
  3. Rename it to index.html (right click, Rename).
Done when it looks like this: a file named index.html is in your Downloads folder. If you double click it, your app opens in the browser.
Why rename? We tested it: Netlify published the file named index.html at once, but a file with spaces in its name got stuck.
9

Publish with Netlify

  1. Open app.netlify.com/signup. Sign up with your school Google account. (First time only.)
  2. Open app.netlify.com/drop.
  3. Drag your index.html file into the box "Drag & drop to publish". Wait until the project page opens.
Done when it looks like this: a project page with a name like incredible-wisp-fcca9c, a green link ending in .netlify.app, and a Private label.
Stuck? If you drop the file without signing in, Netlify puts a password on your site. Sign in first, then drop again.
10

Make it public and copy your link

  1. On the project page, press Make public. New Netlify accounts make every project private at first.
  2. Copy the green link ending in .netlify.app. This is your app link.
  3. Challenge: Project configuration → Change project name, for example w09-himal-cards.
Done when it looks like this: the Private label is gone. Your link opens your app in a new tab.
