Practical Application of AI in Tourism · Week 9

Name Card App Lab

Give AI a few keywords. Get a working name card app with your own link. Anyone can open it on a phone and make a name card.

Basic mission

  • Make your own name card app and get its link.
  • Make one name card with your app and save it as PNG.
  • Post the link and the PNG on Padlet.

Challenge mission

  • Ask Gemini Canvas to build your own version of the app (Step 8).
  • Make a second card for a classmate's business.
